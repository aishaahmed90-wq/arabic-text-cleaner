Remove Extra Spaces from Arabic Text

Clean Arabic text by removing repeated spaces and unnecessary whitespace while keeping the text itself unchanged.

What does this tool do?

This tool removes repeated spaces between words and trims unnecessary spaces from the beginning and end of the text.

It does not change Arabic letters, words, or tashkeel.

Example

Before: هذا    نص   عربي

After: هذا نص عربي
